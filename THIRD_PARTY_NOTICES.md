## Spacecraft trajectories (assets/probes.bin)

Generated by `tools/make_probe_assets.py` from the JPL Horizons system (NASA / Jet Propulsion Laboratory,
Solar System Dynamics Group, https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/horizons/). Positions and velocities were resampled and
thinned out. The Rosetta trajectory in Horizons comes from ESA, the Hayabusa2 trajectory from JAXA navigation; while
Hayabusa2 stays at Ryugu it is replaced by the home position (20 km from Ryugu toward Earth). Use of NASA data does
not imply endorsement by NASA or JPL.

## Milky Way (assets/milkyway.jpg)

Generated by `tools/make_milkyway_asset.py` (downscaled and tone-mapped) from `milkyway_2020_4k.exr` of
*Deep Star Maps 2020* (https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4851/), the Milky Way background without the bright Hipparcos and
Tycho stars.

- Credit: NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio. Gaia DR2: ESA/Gaia/DPAC.
- NASA media, freely reusable including commercial use. Use of NASA data does not imply endorsement by NASA.

## Planet and moon maps (assets/maps.bin)

Generated by `tools/make_planet_maps.py` (resampled to 1024–4096 pixels wide, re-encoded as JPEG/PNG, areas without
data filled with the mean brightness and marked in a coverage mask). Sources, all public domain works of the
U.S. Government (NASA / USGS); use does not imply endorsement by NASA or the USGS:

- Global mosaics from the USGS Astrogeology Science Center planetary map service (https://planetarymaps.usgs.gov/):
  Mercury (MESSENGER MDIS), Mars (Viking MDIM 2.1, colorized by NASA Ames), Ceres (Dawn FC), Vesta (Dawn FC HAMO),
  Io, Europa, Ganymede, Callisto (Galileo SSI / Voyager), Mimas, Enceladus, Tethys, Dione, Rhea, Iapetus (Cassini
  ISS / Voyager), Triton (Voyager 2), Pluto and Charon (New Horizons LORRI / MVIC). Credit: NASA / JPL / USGS and
  the respective mission teams (NASA/JHUAPL/Carnegie Institution of Washington for MESSENGER; NASA/JPL-Caltech/
  UCLA/MPS/DLR/IDA for Dawn; NASA/JPL/Space Science Institute for Cassini; NASA/JHUAPL/SwRI for New Horizons).
- Mars elevation: Mars Global Surveyor MOLA Mission Experiment Gridded Data Record (MEGDR), 4 pixels per degree,
  NASA PDS Geosciences Node (https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/mgs/megdr.html).

## Comet and asteroid shapes (assets/shapes.bin)

Generated by `tools/make_shape_assets.py`. Modifications: vertex coordinates rounded to 16-bit integers, triangle
orientation made consistent, and a table of the outermost surface radius in each direction added. This file is
not covered by this project's MIT license.

- Ryugu: shape model SHAPE_SFM_49k_v20180804 of Watanabe et al. (2019), "Hayabusa2 arrives at the carbonaceous
  asteroid 162173 Ryugu—A spinning top–shaped rubble pile", Science 364, 268–272. Source: ISAS/JAXA
  (https://data.darts.isas.jaxa.jp/pub/hayabusa2/paper/Watanabe_2019/), used under the ISAS Data Policy
  (https://www.isas.jaxa.jp/en/researchers/data-policy/). The data were modified as described above.
- Comet 67P/Churyumov-Gerasimenko: ESA NAVCAM shape model MTP019, low-resolution version
  (CSHP_DV_130_01_LORES), produced by the ESA Rosetta Flight Dynamics team, obtained from the NASA PDS Small Bodies
  Node (ro-c-multi-5-67p-shape-v2.0). Credit: ESA/Rosetta/NAVCAM – CC BY-SA IGO 3.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/igo/). The data were modified as described above, and this
  adapted part of assets/shapes.bin is distributed under the same CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO license. ESA does not endorse
  this project or the modifications.
